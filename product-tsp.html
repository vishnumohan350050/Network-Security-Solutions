Threat Sharing Platform

A centralized hub for collecting, enriching and securely sharing cyber threat intelligence across trusted organizations.

● Under active development

Overview

The Threat Sharing Platform (TSP) automates the collection, enrichment, analysis and distribution of cyber threat intelligence among trusted entities. It integrates data from open-source, commercial and organizational sources into one standardized, reliable exchange.

At the central level, TSP acts as a unified hub aggregating and distributing threat intelligence across organizations. At the organizational level, a Threat Management System (TMS) consumes that shared intelligence to support internal threat analysis, incident response and mitigation using existing security tools.

Key Capabilities

  • Manual threat entry and automatic threat ingestion
  • Threat voting to validate and prioritize shared intelligence
  • Central hub (TSP) feeding organization-level Threat Management Systems (TMS)
  • Secure, controlled intelligence exchange across trusted teams — in development
