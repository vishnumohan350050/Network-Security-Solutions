Threat Sharing Platform (TSP)

A centralized hub for collecting, enriching and securely sharing cyber threat intelligence across trusted organizations.

● Live — expanding with AI-driven automation

Overview

The Threat Sharing Platform (TSP) automates the collection, enrichment, analysis and distribution of cyber threat intelligence among trusted entities, integrating data from open-source, commercial and organizational sources into one standardized exchange.

At the central level, TSP acts as a unified hub aggregating and distributing threat intelligence across organizations. At the organizational level, a Threat Management System (TMS) consumes that shared intelligence to support internal threat analysis, incident response and mitigation using existing security tools.

Key Capabilities

Live today
  • Dynamic template management for threats, security advisories and security news
  • Template-based manual entry plus automated ingestion via configurable workflows
  • Community-based voting and review of manually entered threats before publication
  • Secure threat intelligence sharing across trusted organizations
  • Secure authentication with role-based access control (RBAC)
Planned enhancements
  • Agentic AI-based intelligent crawling of vendor security advisories
  • Threat triaging and risk-score calculation based on relevance
  • Deeper integration with IoT NMS and the Orchestrator for inventory-aware threat sharing
