IoT Security Policy Management System

Vendor-agnostic, template-based policy management for users, devices and applications network-wide.

● Available

Overview

Vendor- and technology-agnostic policy management is essential for network-wide security across devices, solutions and users. The IoT Security Policy Management System implements a centralized system to create and maintain high- and low-level security policies for users, devices and applications.

A Policy Template Management feature lets administrators define reusable templates with configurable attributes, rules and conditions, with rule-based validation and dynamic attribute handling to simplify policy creation — backed by an interactive dashboard for visualizing policies and managing the full policy lifecycle in real time.

Key Capabilities

Capabilities
  • Policy Template Management — reusable templates with configurable attributes, rules and conditions
  • Rule-based validation and dynamic attribute handling
  • Dynamic OpenAPI specification generation for external integration
  • Subscription-based interfacing — external orchestrators/clients subscribe to policy updates
  • Frameworks and Controls Management, mapping controls to policies for structured compliance
Standards work
  • TSDSI study proposal “Study on Standardization of IoT Network Security Policies” initiated, to define a standardized, interoperable policy model for heterogeneous IoT environments
